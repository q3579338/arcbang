Playing

No

Does playing cost anything?

Detonating runs in your own browser. Look at as many universes as you want; it costs nothing.

Not to look

Do I need a wallet?

Only if you want to keep one of them as an NFT. Browsing needs nothing at all.

Wrong number of dimensions

Why did my universe die so fast?

D is how many directions space has, and ours is 3. Hashes hand out 9, 14 or 18 all the time. At those numbers gravity is too steep for a planet to hold an orbit or an electron to hold onto a nucleus, and the whole thing is over in the first second. More than nine blocks in ten end this way, which is why finding one that grows stars is worth something.

Computed

Is the art drawn or computed?

No artist, no layers, no asset library. Every image is a simulation of that universe's own parameters — turn it and you see something else, change one constant and the picture changes with it.

Minting and selling

First 387 are free

What does a mint cost?

The first 387 are free, one per address, gas only. Gas on Arc is USDC, so a mint is a fraction of a cent and you never need a separate fuel token. After that it is 1 USDC, and any one address can take at most 3 more. There are 1,387 in total, then minting stops.

Yes

Can I sell what I mint?

It is a plain ERC-721, so list it on OpenSea. Royalties are 5%. The site also has a simple listing and buying page, with the fee ceiling written into the contract.

Bots

Why is the paid mint limited to 3?

1 USDC is cheap enough that a script could take the whole supply in minutes, and everyone else would be left buying on the secondary market. Three keeps the first mint within reach of more people. It is not there to make anyone feel rushed.

Everything else

The thing exists first

How is this different from other NFT projects?

Most of them sell you a spot and show the art later. Here the thing you want is already on your screen before you pay anything, and looking is free. The scarcity is not a number I picked either: one block hash yields one universe, and once someone mints it, that one is gone.

Open source

Where is the code?

The engine is AGPL-3.0 at github.com/q3579338/arcbang. Any NFT can be recomputed on your own machine and compared against the chain — two commands, listed on verify.

No

Will there be a token?

No token, no presale, no airdrop, no points to farm. Just the 1,387 universes, and when they are minted this is finished.
